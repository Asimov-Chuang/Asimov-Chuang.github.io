## 🎙️ Vocal Performance: Beatbox & A Cappella

Outside of research, I'm deeply involved in **vocal performance**, combining the rhythmic complexity of **beatboxing** with the harmonic beauty of **a cappella**.

As a vocal percussionist and arranger, I enjoy crafting full musical textures using only the human voice — from kick drums and snares to vocal harmonies and melodic leads. This blend allows me to explore sound not just as music, but as structure, emotion, and improvisation.

At **CUHK-Shenzhen**, I was a core member of the university’s a cappella group, where I contributed both as a beatboxer and as a vocal arranger. We rehearsed regularly and performed at campus-wide events, sharing music crafted entirely through voice. During my time as a visiting student at the **University of Pennsylvania**, I joined **Enchord**, one of Penn's premier student-run a cappella groups. There, I expanded my performance experience through multi-part vocal harmonies, complex arrangements, and collaborative live shows.
